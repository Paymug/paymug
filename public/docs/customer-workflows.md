# Customer workflows

## Sign in

Use the customer sign-in link or password flow provided by the store. Do not use
the seller's dashboard credentials.

## Access a purchase

1. Sign in to the customer portal.
2. Open Purchases or Orders.
3. Open the order.
4. Download the file or open the license/GitHub access action.

Access belongs to the store that received the purchase. If the order is missing,
confirm the email address, store URL, payment status, and test/live mode.

## Change preferences

Use the customer account or email-preferences page to change password, profile,
and campaign preferences. An unsubscribe link only changes email subscription; it
does not refund an order or revoke a product entitlement.
