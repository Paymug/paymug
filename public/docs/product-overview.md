# Product overview

Paymug helps a seller sell digital products and subscriptions. A seller connects
PayPal or Stripe. The buyer pays the seller through that provider. Paymug records
the order and can deliver files, license keys, or private GitHub access.

Main areas:

- Storefront and product pages
- One-time products and subscriptions
- PayPal and Stripe payments
- Orders, customers, and customer portal
- Product files, licenses, and GitHub access
- Discounts, email campaigns, subscribers, and automations
- Affiliates and payouts
- API keys and outbound webhooks

Paymug has test and live environments. Keep test data and live data separate.
Always test payment and delivery steps before using live credentials.

Paymug is not the merchant of record. The seller connects their own payment
account and receives the payment through that provider.
