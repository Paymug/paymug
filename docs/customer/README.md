# Customer help

Use the customer portal to sign in, view purchases, download files, use license
keys, cancel subscriptions, manage account details, and change email preferences.

## Cancel a subscription

1. Sign in to the customer portal and open Subscriptions.
2. Select Cancel subscription on the plan, then confirm.

Billing stops immediately and the plan shows as cancelled. This cannot be
undone; to subscribe again, buy the plan again from the store. The button only
shows for plans that are active, trialing, past due or paused. If it is missing
or cancelling fails, contact the store owner.

If a purchase is missing, confirm the email address, store URL, payment status,
and test/live environment. Contact the store owner for payment or access help.

Read the [AI customer workflow guide](../ai/customer-workflows.md) for a short
support checklist.
